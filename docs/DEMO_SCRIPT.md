# GameTechMx Frontier — Demo Scripts

## 30-second demo

**0:00–0:04** — Home screen. “Space missions are systems of connected decisions. GameTechMx Frontier makes those consequences visible.”

**0:04–0:11** — Select Earth Guardian, SSO, and a multispectral instrument. “Choose an objective, orbit, payload, spacecraft, and launcher.”

**0:11–0:17** — Add an instrument; point to mass, power, data, and budget changing together. “Every choice updates every resource immediately and explains the trade-off.”

**0:17–0:23** — Launch and show a solar-storm decision. “Fly the same reproducible mission and respond to real operational tensions.”

**0:23–0:30** — Final score and redesign button. “The debrief says what worked, what failed, and why—so teams can redesign, negotiate, and try again.”

## 2-minute demo

**0:00–0:12 — Problem and promise**  
Show the landing screen. “A science instrument is only useful if the spacecraft can launch it, power it, store its data, and talk to Earth. GameTechMx Frontier teaches that whole system through play.”

**0:12–0:30 — Briefing and design**  
Choose Earth Guardian and SSO. “The briefing defines coverage, resolution, data, lifetime, schedule, and budget. We choose a matching orbit and instrument, then assemble each required subsystem.”

**0:30–0:47 — Visible consequences**  
Add a data-heavy instrument and highlight the data warning. “Our payload now generates more than the S-band link can return. The game quantifies the loss and recommends a stronger band or expanded ground network. Notice that the same change also affects mass, energy, and cost.” Upgrade communications.

**0:47–1:02 — Review**  
Show PDR/CDR checklist. “Before launch we review capacity, eclipse energy, data balance, budget, and objective fit. The formulas are intentionally simple and documented, so learners can explain them.”

**1:02–1:22 — Flight**  
Launch. Accelerate the timeline and pause at an event. “The seed makes every event reproducible. Entering safe mode protects health but costs observing days; continuing protects the schedule while risking the spacecraft.” Choose a response.

**1:22–1:39 — Causal debrief**  
Show the report, chart, requirements, and score bars. “The report scores objective completion, science, efficiency, robustness, and collaboration. It connects failures to actual margins and recommends the next design change.”

**1:39–1:54 — Team play**  
Show hot-seat setup, roles, and a proposal. “Two to four people can play locally with no server. Online rooms use the same roles, proposals, strict-majority votes, chat, and decision log.”

**1:54–2:00 — Close**  
Click Redesign. “Keep the previous result, change one decision, and compare. Design. Negotiate. Explore. This is GameTechMx Frontier.”
